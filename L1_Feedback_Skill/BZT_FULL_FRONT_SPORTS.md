# Shot Type: Full Front

## Definition

A full-body, front-facing shot that captures the model from head to toe. This shot provides a comprehensive view of the entire outfit, including all layers, accessories, and footwear, presented in a clean, commercial format.

***

## Framing

* **Coverage:** Top of head to feet/shoes — full body with a consistent buffer of space above the head and below the footwear
* **Orientation:** Vertical portrait
* **Subject position:** Centered in frame with equal margins on the left and right
* **Camera angle:** Eye-level, shot straight-on with no vertical tilt

***

## Key Characteristics

* Model is oriented directly toward the camera with the torso and hips squared to the lens
* Entire silhouette is visible, ensuring no part of the garment or footwear is cropped
* Consistent "breathing room" maintained at the top and bottom of the frame to prevent the model from touching the edges
* Clean, high-key neutral background (white) with minimal floor shadows
* Posing is natural and functional; arms may hang at sides or hold a relevant accessory (e.g., sports equipment)
* All front-facing garment details—including logos, necklines, and fabric textures—are sharp and legible
* Footwear is fully included, showcasing the break of the trouser or the length of the sock

***

## Purpose

* Serve as the primary "hero" image for e-commerce product detail pages (PDP)
* Provide a clear, unobstructed overview of the complete styled look and garment proportions
* Allow customers to evaluate the fit and compatibility of multiple items within a single outfit

***

## Use Case

This shot type is ideal for:

* Primary listing images on e-commerce category and product pages
* Lookbook layouts where the full head-to-toe styling must be documented
* Technical sportswear catalogs where the relationship between base layers, outerwear, and accessories is critical
* Marketing assets requiring a clean, standardized representation of the product for digital or print use
