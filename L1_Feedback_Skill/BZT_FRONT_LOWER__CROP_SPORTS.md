# Shot Type: Lower Body Front Crop

## Definition

A lower-body focused shot that captures the model from the mid-torso to the feet. This framing prioritizes the fit, technical construction, and length of bottoms while providing performance context through the inclusion of footwear and relevant props.

***

## Framing

* **Coverage:** Mid-torso (above the waistband) to the floor/feet.
* **Orientation:** Vertical portrait
* **Subject position:** Centered in frame, primarily front-facing with occasional slight rotation for dynamic poses
* **Camera angle:** Waist-level, shot straight-on to ensure accurate representation of leg length and garment proportions

***

## Key Characteristics

* The waistband of the lower garment is fully visible, clearly showing the rise, closure details, or elastic construction
* The entire length of the garment is captured, including hemlines, side seams, and specialized cuffs or inner linings
* The upper body is cropped at the mid-torso, typically showing the navel or the bottom edge of a top to provide a clear view of the waist fit.
* Footwear and socks are included in the frame to provide a complete styling reference and ground the model
* Performance props (e.g., yoga mats, sports balls, golf clubs, or weights) are frequently used to signal the product's functional category
* Posing is active or athletic—such as kneeling, stepping, or holding equipment—to demonstrate fabric stretch and mobility
* Garment details like technical seams, logos, pocket placements, and fabric texture remain sharp and highly legible
* Clean, neutral studio background with minimal shadows to maintain focus on the product's silhouette

***

## Purpose

* Highlight the specific fit, drape, and technical features of shorts, leggings, and performance trousers
* Communicate the intended use case of the garment by incorporating sport-specific equipment and styling
* Demonstrate how the fabric behaves under tension or during movement through dynamic, performance-oriented posing

***

## Use Case

This shot type is ideal for:

* Primary or secondary Product Detail Page (PDP) imagery for activewear bottoms, gym shorts, and technical trousers
* E-commerce listings for performance gear where the relationship between the garment and sports equipment is a key selling point
* Mobile-first shopping platforms where a tighter lower-body crop allows for better visibility of product details compared to a full-body shot
* Technical lookbooks and digital catalogs that emphasize fabric performance and functional design elements
