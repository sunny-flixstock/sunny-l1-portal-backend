# Shot Type: Front Crop (Upper)

## Definition

A mid-length shot capturing the model from the top of the head to the mid-thigh, emphasizing the garment's fit and performance during athletic movement. This framing highlights the upper body silhouette and technical details while providing functional context through dynamic posing or the inclusion of sports equipment.

***

## Framing

* **Coverage:** Top of head to mid-thigh — the lower crop falls between the hip and the knee
* **Orientation:** Vertical portrait
* **Subject position:** Generally centered, allowing for limb extension and dynamic stances within the frame
* **Camera angle:** Eye-level to chest-level, shot straight-on to maintain garment proportions

***

## Key Characteristics

* Model is captured in active, sport-specific poses (e.g., stretching, running, or holding equipment) to demonstrate fabric stretch and range of motion
* The entire upper garment is visible, including the neckline, armholes, and hemline
* The frame cuts the lower garment at the upper thigh — the garment itself continues at full length out of frame; only the upper thigh area is visible
* Technical details such as mesh paneling, perforations, heat-sealed logos, and moisture-wicking textures are rendered with high clarity
* Headwear and accessories (caps, visors, sunglasses, gloves) are fully included to complete the performance look
* Arms may be raised or bent, but must not obscure primary branding or key construction features of the torso
* Lighting is high-contrast and directional to emphasize muscle definition and the technical drape of the fabric
* Clean, neutral background (white) to keep the focus entirely on the product and movement

***

## Purpose

* Demonstrate how the garment moves, stretches, and fits the body during high-intensity physical activity
* Provide a lifestyle-oriented perspective that connects the product to specific athletic use cases (e.g., tennis, golf, training)
* Highlight the integration of technical features and branding in a way that feels functional rather than static

***

## Use Case

This shot type is ideal for:

* Primary Product Detail Page (PDP) imagery for performance tops, jerseys, sports bras, and technical outerwear
* Sport-specific category landing pages where movement and equipment provide necessary consumer context
* Mobile-optimized e-commerce interfaces where a tighter crop ensures technical details are visible without zooming
* Digital lookbooks and marketing assets that prioritize the "athleisure" or "performance" identity of the brand
