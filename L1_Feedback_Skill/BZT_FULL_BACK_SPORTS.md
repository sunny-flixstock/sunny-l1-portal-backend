# Shot Type: BZT Full Back

## Definition

Full-body rear shot capturing the model from head to toe. Focuses on back silhouette, construction, and technical design details.

***

## Framing

* **Coverage:** Head to feet — full body
* **Orientation:** Vertical portrait
* **Subject position:** Centered, back oriented toward the lens
* **Camera angle:** Eye-level, shot straight-on with no vertical tilt

***

## Key Characteristics

* Model's back faces camera; head will be in profile
* Entire outfit visible from nape to footwear
* Arms clear of the torso to show side/back seams
* Highlights technical details: racerbacks, hoods, and waistbands
* Balanced framing with consistent head/foot margins
* Clean studio lighting to define fabric texture and seams

***

## Purpose

* Showcase rear technical construction and strap designs
* Illustrate full-body silhouette and garment drape
* Provide essential visual data for rear-facing product details

***

## Use Case

Standard for activewear listings to show functional design:

* Detailing sports bra strap configurations
* Displaying rear branding or ventilation panels
* Showing short/legging length and seat fit
* Completing 360-degree product coverage
